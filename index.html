See it in action
Everything you need, nothing you don't

From lock screen alert to detailed forecast, spot chat to kite size recommendation — here's what WindChaser looks like in the wild.

🔔 Lock screen alert

Push alerts land on your lock screen at 7am on the mornings that the conditions line up — no need to check the forecast yourself.

📧 Alert email

Email alerts give you the full picture: spot name, wind speed and direction, and exactly how many good hours to expect — and when they start.

🏄 My Spots dashboard

Your spots at a glance — colour-coded hours of good conditions across today, tomorrow, and the day after. Green means go.

📊 7-day forecast

Wind speed, gusts, direction, recommended kite size, tide height, waves, and temperature — all colour-coded to your personal settings.

🧭 Wind direction compass

Set which directions work at your spot across four tiers: Perfect, Good, Poor, and Dangerous. The forecast is scored against your settings, not a generic average.

💬 Spot chat

Each spot has its own community chat. Share real conditions, coordinate sessions, and connect with local riders.

🗺️ Kite spots map

Browse a growing map of community-contributed kite spots around the UK coastline, or add your own favourite beach anywhere in the world.

⚙️ Your profile

Set your weight for accurate kite size recommendations, your wind range, and exactly which mornings, afternoons, and evenings you're free to kite.

🆘 Built-in help

In-app help covers everything from setting up alerts and installing on your home screen to adding new spots and understanding the forecast.

From the creator
Why I built this

Hi. My name is Ken, and I am the creator of WindChaser. It started as a passion project, just for me. I live on the North East coast in England, and the wind blows offshore almost all of the time — so much so that I gave up scanning weather apps.

And then one day I went for a walk on the beach and the wind was stonking onshore, the lagoons were perfect and the sun was shining. And I almost missed a fantastic session. So I thought — an app that alerts me when the conditions are right would be great.

Building it turned out to be more complex than I expected. But I made two commitments when I started, and I'm keeping them: no ads, ever — and it will never cost more than a cup of coffee a month.
